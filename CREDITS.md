# Halo 3 SPR Assets: credits

**Halo 3 SPR Assets** is a free Forge asset pack for Halo 3 on Project Reclaimer. It holds 85 vehicles and weapons:
the "SPR" versions played on the SPR servers, each named "<Name> SPR" in Forge, so any map can offer them. SPR
publishes it on the Steam Workshop (unlisted), with a public source repository.

Very little of it starts with us. Every item comes from **HugeGrass SPR**, our tuned build of **RejectedShotgun's
HugeGrass**, and most of HugeGrass's vehicles are RejectedShotgun's own work or his ports. Under that sits **Bungie's
Halo 3**, and the work of **J-S**, **Skaboodlydoodle**, **VKMT and the R3ACH team**, and **wert-ac**. This file says
who made what, which SPR items use it, where to find the original, and the terms each author published, quoted as
they are.

Researched on 23 November 2026. Anything we couldn't confirm is marked **(unconfirmed)**.

## RejectedShotgun (RJShotgun)

**RejectedShotgun** made HugeGrass, the map every SPR item comes from, and most of the vehicles in it: his own
vehicles, his ports of other Halo games' assets to Halo 3, and his reworks of other authors' vehicles. Thank you,
RJ: none of this exists without you.

- Steam: [RejectedShotgun](https://steamcommunity.com/id/rjshotgun), his collection
  [RejectedShotgun Maps](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3008970180)
- GitHub: [RJShotgun](https://github.com/RJShotgun)

### HugeGrass

- **Hugegrass** on the Steam Workshop: <https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2984061723>
  (version 3.0, map file `hugeass_remastered_mcc`). Its page describes it as "an update to Hugegrass with many new
  reworks, balance changes, new vehicles, new weapons, expanded Forge, improved visuals, and much more designed
  specifically to be played on Project Reclaimer".
- Its own credits, as written on its page: "Plasma Launcher (Credit: VKMT)", "Focus Rifle (Credit: VKMT)",
  "Sparrowhawk (Created by Skaboodlydoodle)", "Vulture (Created by J-S)".
- **HugeGrass SPR** (<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3832239928>) is SPR's build of it, made
  with his blessing. Every item in this pack is HugeGrass SPR's, as built in its v3.0.SPR.1.2.

### His tag repository

- **RejectedShotgun-Tags**: <https://github.com/RJShotgun/RejectedShotgun-Tags>, "Tag files, soundbanks, and data
  files for Halo 1/2/3/ODST/Reach". We use it at commit `dc29731` (8 June 2026).
- The source recipes for Shadow SPR, Lich SPR, ODST Pod SPR, Seraph SPR, Skiff SPR, Fast Skiff SPR, Space Banshee
  SPR, Spectre SPR, AA Spectre SPR and Fast AA Ghost SPR start from it. Until a few tags they need are published (or,
  for the Shadow, until its physics build matches), those items are copied compiled from HugeGrass SPR. His README says: "Some tag references may be
  missing from this repository. Create an issue for any you find and I will add the missing tags."
- Several of the pack's sound banks are his (Lich, MAC cannon and others; see [Sound banks](#sound-banks)).

### What in this pack is his

- **His ports of Halo: Reach assets** (Reach by Bungie): Sabre SPR, Falcon SPR, Falcon GL SPR, Fast Falcon GL SPR,
  Spirit SPR, Space Banshee SPR, Space Phantom SPR, Reach Seraph SPR, Reach Shade SPR.
- **Bungie's Halo 3 and Halo 3: ODST assets, made playable or reworked by him:** Seraph SPR (Halo 3's Seraph, made
  flyable), Pelican SPR, Phantom SPR, Troop Warthog SPR, Anti-Air Wraith SPR, Gravity Throne SPR, ODST Pod SPR, Car
  SPR, Police Car SPR, Gauss Turret SPR.
- **His own vehicles and variants:** AA Ghost SPR, Fast AA Ghost SPR, Spectre SPR, AA Spectre SPR, Fast AA Spectre
  SPR, Shadow SPR, Skiff SPR, Fast Skiff SPR, Lich SPR, Condor SPR, Mammoth SPR, Wombat SPR, Longsword SPR, Civilian
  Warthog SPR, Rocket Warthog SPR, Fast Rocket Warthog SPR, Fast AA Wraith SPR, and the rocket turret on Warthog SPR,
  Gauss Warthog SPR, Wrecked Warthog SPR, Fast Warthog SPR and Fast Gauss Warthog SPR. The "fast" handling of Fast Scorpion SPR, Fast Hornet
  SPR, Fast Warthog SPR and Fast Gauss Warthog SPR is his ("All Vanilla Vehicles but faster", on his page).
- **His weapons and weapon variants:** Power Plasma Pistol SPR, Red Assault Rifle SPR, Rocket Launcher H2 SPR, the
  tracking version of the Plasma Launcher (Plasma Launcher SPR), and the Focus Rifle's beam damage (Focus Rifle SPR).
- **His reworks of other authors' vehicles:** the Albatross (wert-ac's; Albatross SPR), and his changes to J-S's
  Vulture (Vulture SPR: a missile explosion and a light-volume shader of his) and Kestrel (Kestrel SPR is his fast
  railgun variant).
- **His changes to stock Halo 3 tags inside HugeGrass**, which SPR keeps so the items play as they do on HugeGrass
  SPR: the Wraith's anti-infantry projectile effect at high priority, the red Plasma Rifle without a level-of-detail
  model, the Hornet's right boost sound at gain 0, and a plasma ring particle drawn double-sided, which sits under
  the impact effects of most weapons.
- Pilot Gun Pelican SPR, Fast Troop Warthog SPR and Fast Civilian Warthog SPR are SPR's variants of his vehicles.

In all, 54 of the 85 items include his work (see the [table](#item--origin--authors)), and every one of the 85
comes through his map.

  the ODST Car and Police Car, Falcons, Wombat, Sabre, Seraphs, Pelican, Phantom, Space Phantom, Spirit, Longsword,
  Condor, Lich, Albatross, ODST Drop Pod and Mammoth. Anti-Air Wraith SPR's base keeps its Ultimate Forge name
  (`wraith_anti_air_uf`).
- Its page credits "Sparrowhawk (Made by Skaboodlydoodle)", "Sparrowhawk, Chaingun (Made by Skaboodlydoodle)" and
  "Vulture (Made by J-S)". We found no other Ultimate Forge 2.0 authors (unconfirmed: Steam lists only RejectedShotgun

### Permafrost

- **Permafrost** on the Steam Workshop: <https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3663541185>, also his.
  HugeGrass SPR took the R3ACH sound bank `sfx.r3ach` from it, and this pack ships that bank (byte for byte the same
  file as in the R3ACH 1.0.2 release). Its page credits "Focus Rifle (By VKMT)", "Plasma Launcher (By VKMT)" and
  "Kestrel (By J-S)".

### Terms

- His repository has no licence file. Its README says: "I do ask that you credit me for the many, many hours I have
  put in to creating these. It's not a requirement, but would be much appreciated :)"
- He gave SPR his blessing to publish HugeGrass SPR (2 November 2026), and that blessing covers all of his work,
  this pack included.

## Bungie

**Bungie** made Halo 3 and its vehicles and weapons, and Halo 3: ODST, Halo: Reach and Halo 2, whose assets
RejectedShotgun and the R3ACH team ported.

- **Halo 3's own vehicles and weapons**, from the Halo 3 Editing Kit's stock tags, are the base of 31 items built
  from source: Banshee SPR, Chopper SPR, Hornet SPR, Ghost SPR, Prowler SPR, Mongoose SPR, Scorpion SPR, Wraith SPR,
  Hornet Lite SPR, Shade SPR, Fast Scorpion SPR, Fast Hornet SPR, Fast Mongoose SPR, Spartan Laser SPR, Missile Pod
  SPR, Plasma Pistol SPR, Machine Gun Turret SPR, Plasma Cannon SPR, Assault Rifle SPR, Battle Rifle SPR, Carbine SPR,
  Plasma Rifle SPR, Red Plasma Rifle SPR, Magnum SPR, Automag SPR, Spiker SPR, Needler SPR, Brute Shot SPR, Fuel Rod
  Gun SPR, Beam Rifle SPR and Sentinel Beam SPR.
- **Halo 3's Warthogs, Pelican, Phantom, Wraith, Ghost, Gauss turret, Assault Rifle, Plasma Pistol and Rocket
  Launcher** also sit under RJ's versions: the Warthog family, Pelican SPR, Pilot Gun Pelican SPR, Phantom SPR, Troop
  Warthog SPR, Anti-Air Wraith SPR, Fast AA Wraith SPR, AA Ghost SPR, Fast AA Ghost SPR, Gauss Turret SPR, Red
  Assault Rifle SPR, Power Plasma Pistol SPR and Rocket Launcher H2 SPR. Halo 3's Seraph and Gravity Throne are the
  base of Seraph SPR and Gravity Throne SPR.
- **Halo 3: ODST's** civilian car, drop pod and Phantom parts: Car SPR, Police Car SPR, ODST Pod SPR, Phantom SPR.

## Item → origin → author(s)

All 85 items, in the order of HugeGrass SPR's Forge menus. "SPR" in every row is SPR's tuning; every item comes
through RJ's HugeGrass. "In this build" is how the 23 November 2026 build makes each item: from source tags, or
copied compiled from HugeGrass SPR until source is published (34 from source, 51 copied).

vehicles' own models, so their authors are the vehicle's:
- Falcon SPR, Falcon GL SPR and Fast Falcon GL SPR are offered as eight entries: Falcon SPR, Falcon GL SPR, Falcon Chin
  Gun SPR, Falcon Chin Gun GL SPR, and the Fast version of each (RejectedShotgun's 9 seats in all eight).
- Phantom SPR's setups: Plasma Cannon Phantom SPR, Searchlight Phantom SPR, Unarmed Phantom SPR, Side Cannon Phantom SPR.
- Gauss Turret SPR's: Chaingun Turret SPR.
- Warthog SPR's: Unarmed Warthog SPR, Standard-Paint Rocket Warthog SPR. Rocket Warthog SPR's: Rocket-Paint Warthog SPR,
  Rocket-Paint Gauss Warthog SPR. Civilian Warthog SPR's: Civilian Gauss Warthog SPR.
- Fast AA Spectre SPR's: Fast Spectre SPR. Wraith SPR's: Flak Wraith SPR. Fast AA Wraith SPR's: Fast Minigun Wraith
  SPR. Scorpion SPR's: Wrecked Scorpion SPR.

## Tools

- **Halo 3 Editing Kit (H3EK)**: <https://store.steampowered.com/app/1695791/>. Its stock tags are the source of the
  31 Halo 3 items. Its `tool.exe` compiles the source-built items (`build-cache-file`), writes and reads back the SPR
  values (`patch-tag-field`, `export-tag-to-xml`) and makes missing shader templates. Its ManagedBlam library writes
  the fields `tool.exe` can't reach, through a small program of ours. Its `7zr.exe` unpacked the kit and the
  downloaded releases.
- **The other MCC Editing Kits** (Halo 3: ODST, Halo: Reach, Halo 2, Halo 2 Anniversary MP, Halo: Combat Evolved):
  their file listings were read to trace which game each ported asset came from. Nothing from them is in the pack.
- **Project Reclaimer**: <https://projectreclaimer.dev>, releases at
  <https://github.com/ProjectReclaimer/project-reclaimer-releases>. Its Forge offers a mod's vehicles and weapons on
  any map, which is what this pack is for. Version 0.10.9's `--kit-tool verify` checks every build, its Reclaimer Kit
  built the first test packs, and its dedicated servers ran the tests.
- **Assembly**: <https://github.com/XboxChaos/Assembly> (GNU GPL v3.0), the Blam engine research tool by Xbox Chaos
  and its contributors (most commits: Lord-Zedd, adierking, 0xdeafcafe). Its Halo 3 MCC plugins, the field layouts
  written by Lord Zedd, -DeToX-, DeadCanadian, DarkShallFall, Veegie and others named in them, are how our tools read
  every field of a compiled map. Our tag copier learned how resources are injected from Assembly's
  TagContainerInjector (`src/Blamite/Injection/TagContainerInjector.cs`). Assembly's plugin files keep their GPL v3.0
  licence wherever they are shared.
- **Excession**, MCC's Steam Workshop publishing tool: our upload script uses its libraries and its verifier.
- **7-Zip** (`7zr.exe`, as shipped with H3EK): <https://www.7-zip.org/>, by Igor Pavlov.
- **Python**, **NumPy** and **Pillow** run SPR's own build tools. The Workshop preview image is rendered by them from
  the H3EK source tags of the pack's own vehicles (`tools\preview`), with the textures their authors made.

## SPR

**SPR**, a community server group, made this pack for the SPR servers and publishes it.

- **The SPR values:** each vehicle's and weapon's speeds, masses, armour, weapon reach, aim assist and lock-on ranges,
  tuned by Halo lore, kept as data files with a reason for each.
- **The fixes** applied before the values: fitted flip spheres, fade distances, lock-on fixes and others.
- **New tags:** the crash and collision damage tags (`custom\spr_collision\...`), Lich SPR's own model copy (without
  an unfinished extra variant), the eight Falcon configurations and the other 14 setups made vehicles of their own, a
  one-setup model for every other vehicle, Pilot Gun Pelican SPR, the fast copies (Fast Mongoose SPR, Fast Troop
  Warthog SPR, Fast Civilian Warthog SPR).
- **The Reach Shade's five sound banks**, rebuilt from Halo: Reach's own audio so the Reach Shade isn't silent.
- **The build tools**, which take the values out of HugeGrass SPR, write them into source tags, compile the pack and
  check it field by field against HugeGrass SPR, and the testing on the SPR servers.
